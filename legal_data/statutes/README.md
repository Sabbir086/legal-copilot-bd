# Bangladesh statutory corpus

`sources.json` currently contains two placeholder Contract Act records only. They are explicitly unverified discovery placeholders and must not be used as authoritative legal text.

Do not change a record to verified merely because its title or URL exists. Import the exact text from an official primary source, preserve its source/version metadata, reconcile amendments and commencement dates, validate repeal/current status, and record the verification method/date first.
